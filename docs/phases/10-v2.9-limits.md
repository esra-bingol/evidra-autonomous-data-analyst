## Sınırlar (`analysis/limits.py`)

| Env | Varsayılan |
| --- | --- |
| `EVIDRA_MAX_UPLOAD_BYTES` | 10 MiB |
| `EVIDRA_MAX_UPLOAD_ROWS` | 50_000 |
| `EVIDRA_MAX_UPLOAD_COLUMNS` | 64 |
| `EVIDRA_MAX_UPLOAD_CELL_LENGTH` | 4096 |
| `EVIDRA_MAX_ZIP_UNCOMPRESSED_BYTES` | 10 MiB |
| `EVIDRA_MAX_ZIP_MEMBERS` | 16 |

Byte: Content-Length (slack ile) + chunked yazım. 413. Satır/kolon/hücre ve unsafe filename: 400. ZIP uncompressed: 413.
